About

I am a private trader. I study forex price action and test trading strategies on historical and live market data in a personal research tool that I built for myself.

Strategie Lab Bot

Strategie Lab Bot is a personal application that connects to my own trading account through the cTrader Open API.

  • It places orders only on my own account (currently an IC Markets demo account), based on signals from my own strategy.
  • It runs locally on my own computer. There is no public service and no other users.
  • Every order is sent with a stop-loss and take-profit, and risk per trade is limited in the application settings.
  • It is not offered, sold or shared with anyone else, and it does not manage money for others.
